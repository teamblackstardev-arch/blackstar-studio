Data deletion

To remove your local NEXOPS profile and app data on Android:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Select Apps, then NEXOPS.
  3. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  4. Select Clear storage or Clear data and confirm.

This may permanently remove local progress and preferences. Menu names vary by device. It does not guarantee deletion of data stored remotely or in backups.

For questions or requests involving any other data, contact teamblackstardev@gmail.com. This page does not provide an automated online account deletion service.
