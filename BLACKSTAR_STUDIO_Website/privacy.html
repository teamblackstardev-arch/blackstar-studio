Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy describes the confirmed privacy practices of the NEXOPS Android beta, developed by BLACKSTAR STUDIO.

Player profile and local storage

NEXOPS allows players to enter a username. The developer reports that the username and player profile are stored locally on the device. Email and password registration and third-party sign-in are not currently offered.

Advertising and purchases

The current beta does not display advertisements and does not offer functioning in-app purchases. This policy will be updated before any relevant new data practices are introduced.

Network services and diagnostics

The beta’s multiplayer data flows, third-party SDK behavior and diagnostic data practices have not yet been verified. This policy does not assert that no data leaves the device. Verified details of any collection, sharing, retention and security practices must be added before release.

Local data removal

Players can remove locally stored app data using the Android app storage settings. See our data deletion guide. This does not necessarily delete any data held by external services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact teamblackstardev@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy will be updated when verified data practices or game features change.
